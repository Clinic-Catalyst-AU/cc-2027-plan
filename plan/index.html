01 · The shift

They want it done for them.

Our last build settled it. Owners value the result far more than learning to drive the system, and we delivered hers without being in the same room.

What we stop

  • Multi-day build-it-yourself workshops
  • Whole days on a call while the owner is busy elsewhere
  • Promising extras outside the package
  • Calling Google a "conversion campaign"

What we sell

  • One set-up, $5,500, 30 days, everything in writing
  • One onboarding session, then we build in the background
  • Their own build recorded and cut into a course
  • Works for a clinic in any city, or any country
