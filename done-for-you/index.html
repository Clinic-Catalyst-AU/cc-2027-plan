Common questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We do the building. In your working session and your walk-through we explain each piece, and your training library is there whenever you want to look something up.

Where are you based, and do I need to travel?

We work with clinics anywhere in Australia, online. You stay in your clinic and nobody loses a day to travel.

What do you need from me?

One working session with us, and the short onboarding pack before it: access to your accounts, your photos and a few answers about your clinic.

What happens after the 30 days?

The system is yours and it keeps running. You can run it yourself with your training library, or stay on for $99 a week, which covers maintenance of your systems and your ad reporting. Ad management is not included.

I already have a marketing agency. Can I still do this?

Yes. You will own the accounts and the system either way, and you will be able to see what your marketing is doing. From there you can decide what you still want an agency for.

Is my ad spend included?

Ad spend is separate and paid by you, direct to Meta and Google, so every dollar of it goes into your campaigns. In your working session we talk through a starting budget that suits your clinic.
