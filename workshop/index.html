Why this day exists

You pay for marketing. You deserve to understand it.

Many clinic owners we meet get a report each month and still cannot say which enquiries became patients. Nobody has sat down and explained how the pieces fit, in words that make sense to someone who runs a clinic.

This day does that. You will see a real campaign pulled apart on screen: the offer, the ad, the page it sends people to and the follow-up behind it, with the reason for every choice.

We show you all of it. Take photos all day, go home and build it yourself.

Offer crafting

Meta ads, and the numbers that matter

Open your Facebook and Instagram ad account and know what you are looking at. We show you the numbers that tell you if a campaign is working, and the vanity numbers that fill most reports.

  • The handful of numbers worth checking each week
  • How audiences work today, and when to narrow them
03

Google Ads

How to show up when someone nearby searches for what you offer. Alicia explains broad match, phrase match and exact match, when to use each, and how a search campaign is put together.

  • Match types in plain words
  • A real campaign, explained choice by choice
04

Pixels and tracking

Many clinics have a pixel on their home page and nothing after it. Kelly shows you the piece that is usually missing, and why it is the one that tells you where your bookings came from.

  • What a pixel does and where it belongs
  • How to check your own
05

Landing pages, lead forms and booking links

Where you send someone after they click changes the result. We compare a landing page, a lead form and a direct booking link, and show you when to use each one.

  • What a page needs to show before anyone scrolls
  • Lead form or booking link: how to choose
06

Your CRM and the follow-up

What happens to an enquiry in the first minute, the first day and the first week. Kelly shows you the system we build for clinics and how the follow-up runs while you are with patients.

  • Why we use a CRM and what it replaces
  • Each message a person gets between enquiry and booking

Common questions

Before you come.

Do I need any marketing knowledge?

No. Everything is explained from the start, in plain words. If you can run a clinic, you can follow this day.

Will you build my campaigns on the day?

This is a learning day, so nothing is built in the room. You leave understanding how it all works and able to build it yourself or brief someone who will.

I already have an agency. Is this for me?

Yes. You will understand what they should be doing for you and be able to check that they are doing a good job.

Is it only for aesthetic clinics?

The examples come from skin and aesthetic clinics, because that is where we work. Owners of other booking-based clinics are welcome.
